# MindBridge what-if report (N-1)

Generated by `python -m mindbridge whatif` from `tools/mindbridge/design.yaml` and the Terraform. Every number here comes from the deterministic model; nothing is produced by AI.

Method: N-1: remove each component, evaluate every flow. severity = flow weight x impact x recovery penalty. Recovery penalty: manual 10, provider 4, auto 1 + seconds/60 (cap 5). Bands: high >= 60, medium >= 20.

Components marked **planned** come from `docs/plan.md` (aws/eks, azure/aks, global/dns are not yet in code).

BGP paths are scored for two failure modes, and both are shown. A **clean withdrawal** (config mismatch, admin shutdown, a peer NOTIFICATION, a VPN connection replaced through the API) moves the flows at once. A **silent failure** (instance reboot or crash) leaves the flows on the lost tunnels waiting for the BGP hold timer. The row's severity uses the worse one. The **reduced-redundancy window** is the time a component stays degraded after users have recovered; it adds weight x share lost x min(3, seconds/600) to the severity.

Recovery times marked `source: measured 2026-11-23` come from Session 1 (`docs/runs/test3-summary.md`, `test4-summary.md`, `slowburn-summary.md`).
